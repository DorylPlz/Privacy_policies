# NotificationFilter privacy policy

Policy URL after GitHub Pages is enabled for this repository:

- English and Spanish: <https://dorylplz.github.io/Privacy_policies/NotificationFilter/privacy_policy.html>

The page selects a language based on the browser preference and includes a manual English/Español selector. The app passes its selected language using `?lang=en` or `?lang=es`.

## Before publishing or changing the Play listing

- Confirm the full name of the individual or legal entity publishing the app. Replace the generic developer/controller wording near the top of the policy with the accurate publisher identity. Add any address or other contact details required for the publisher and applicable local law; these details were not present in the source repositories, so they must not be guessed.
- Confirm that `notification.filter.app@gmail.com` is monitored and is the correct privacy contact.
- Enable GitHub Pages for this repository and publish the page. Open the HTTPS URL in a private browser window, without signing in, and verify it loads worldwide, both language options work, and it is readable on mobile.
- Put the policy URL in the Google Play Console privacy-policy field. Ensure the same policy is linked from inside the app.
- Complete and keep current the Play Console Data safety and app-content declarations based on the actual release artifact and every included SDK. Local-only processing is described here; do not use this wording if a future SDK or feature transmits data.
- Keep diagnostic file logging disabled in the Play release unless its collection, retention, and optional export behavior are reviewed and declared accurately.
- Recheck notification access, app-inventory access, network traffic, release settings, and these disclosures whenever app code or dependencies change.

This policy is an operational notice, not legal advice or a guarantee of compliance. In particular, have counsel confirm applicability and required controller identity/contact details for Chile (including Law No. 21,719, scheduled to take effect December 1, 2026) and the U.S. states where the app is offered.
